Who We Are

Meet Sean — Your Local Locksmith in Ipswich

DML Locksmiths is run by Sean, a professional locksmith based right here in Ipswich, Suffolk. Sean set up DML Locksmiths with a straightforward goal: to provide a genuinely reliable, honest locksmith service to the local community — without the inflated prices or poor communication that can sometimes come with larger firms.

Whether you've locked yourself out at an inconvenient hour, need new locks fitting after moving into a property, or simply want the peace of mind of knowing your home is properly secured, Sean is the kind of tradesperson who shows up on time, explains exactly what needs doing, and charges a fair price for the job.

Every customer matters. Sean takes pride in the quality of his work and the relationships he builds with the people he helps across Ipswich and the wider Suffolk area — from first-time callers to families who keep his number saved for whenever they need him.

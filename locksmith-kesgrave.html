Common Lock Issues in Kesgrave

Kesgrave has a strong mix of modern estates, family homes, rentals and high-use uPVC doors — and each can bring its own common lock and door problems.

01

uPVC Multi-Point Lock Problems

Many Kesgrave and Grange Farm homes use uPVC doors with multi-point locking systems. Over time, handles can become stiff, gearboxes can wear and doors can drop slightly — making the lock harder to use or less secure.

02

Move-In Lock Changes

Kesgrave is a popular place for families, first-time buyers and renters, so lock changes are often needed when keys are unaccounted for, tenants move out, or homeowners want better peace of mind.

03

School Run & Commuter Lockouts

Busy mornings, school runs, dog walks and commuter journeys towards Ipswich can easily lead to lost keys or accidental lockouts. Sean can help you regain entry safely and advise whether the lock needs changing afterwards.

04

Misaligned Doors and Stiff Locks

Daily use, changing weather and small shifts in hinges can cause doors to move out of alignment. This puts pressure on the lock, making keys harder to turn and increasing the risk of the mechanism failing completely.
